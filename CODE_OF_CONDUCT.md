# Code of conduct

## In short

Be respectful and constructive. Technical criticism is welcome and expected;
personal attacks are not.

## Expected behaviour

- Engage with the strongest version of someone's argument.
- Prefer evidence — a failing test, a trace, a measurement — over assertion.
- Accept correction gracefully. Finding a bug in this repository is a
  contribution, not an insult.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would
be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull
requests that do not follow this code, and may ban repeat offenders from the
repository. Concerns can be raised through the contact address on the
maintainer's GitHub profile; reports will be handled discreetly.
